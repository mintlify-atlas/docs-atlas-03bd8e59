VRP Solution is a full-stack Two-Echelon Vehicle Routing Problem (2E-VRP) solver designed for urban logistics. Enter your depot, customers, vehicles, and parking hubs — the system computes optimized routes with real-world road geometry, handles last-mile walking segments from hub to customer, and visualizes everything on an interactive map.

## What VRP Solution Does

VRP Solution tackles the **Two-Echelon Vehicle Routing Problem (2E-VRP)**: vehicles drive from a central depot to intermediate parking hubs, then couriers walk the final distance to nearby customers on foot. This mirrors real urban delivery scenarios where large vehicles cannot reach every door.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define your nodes and fleet">
    Add a depot, customer stops (with demand and service time), and your vehicles (with capacity and cost) — manually or by importing a CSV.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select or configure parking stations">
    Choose from pre-configured EZ Parking stations or enable automatic hub generation to let the optimizer place hubs via K-Means clustering.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the optimizer">
    Click **Calculate Routes**. The backend geocodes addresses, builds a distance/duration matrix via OpenRouteService, solves the VRP with OR-Tools, and returns per-vehicle routes with full polyline geometry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Analyze and export results">
    Review per-vehicle driving and walking summaries, stop-by-stop timelines with arrival/departure times, and any unassigned nodes. Export to CSV or open directly in Google Maps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Architecture at a Glance

The application has two services that work together:

- **FastAPI backend** (Python) — handles geocoding, distance matrix computation, VRP solving via OR-Tools, and route geometry via OpenRouteService
- **React frontend** (Vite + Tailwind) — renders the split-panel UI with `react-leaflet` map and all form interactions

See the [Architecture overview](/architecture) for a full breakdown of data flow between services.
